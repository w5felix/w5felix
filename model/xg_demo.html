Static xG demo (model-only)

This page runs the exported xG model entirely in your browser. Train and export on your machine, then serve the folder statically.

  1. Train: python3 archive-2/train_xg_nn.py --train
  2. Export for web: python3 archive-2/train_xg_nn.py --export-web --model-path archive-2/models/xg_mlp.joblib
  3. Serve statically from repo root: python3 -m http.server 8000
  4. Open: http://localhost:8000/archive-2/web/xg_demo.html
StatsBomb units: x in [0,120], y in [0,80]. Click the pitch to predict.
